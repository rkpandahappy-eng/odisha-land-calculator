Privacy Policy – Odisha Land Calculator

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Odisha Land Calculator ("the app") is made by Odi Techno Apps. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no sign-up, no account and no analytics or advertising.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings (language, day / night mode, local units you add and whether you accepted the disclaimer) are saved only on your own phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Calculations

All unit conversions, plot measurements and stamp duty estimates are calculated on your phone. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere.

Links and sharing

The app has links to outside websites such as Bhulekh and IGR Odisha. When you open them, those websites' own privacy policies apply. If you use the Share button, the text is passed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp).

Children

The app is meant for adults dealing with land matters and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
